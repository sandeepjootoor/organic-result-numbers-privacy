Privacy Policy

Organic Result Numbers is a Chrome extension that numbers organic results on supported Google Search pages, highlights an optional address you enter, and lets you export results when you request it.

Information processed in your browser

To provide its features, the extension reads the current Google Search page's query and organic result titles, displayed text, and links in your browser. This information is processed locally to show result positions and, when you choose, to prepare a CSV download or clipboard copy. The extension does not send this information to its developer or to a remote server.

If you enter a domain or page address in the optional rank box, that address is saved in Chrome storage on your device so it can be reused on Google Search pages. It is not sent to a server. You can clear it by emptying the rank box; removing the extension removes its stored value.

Exports

CSV files are created on your device only when you click Export CSV. Result rows are copied to your clipboard only when you click Copy. You control where downloaded files are kept and how copied content is used.

Data sharing and advertising

The extension does not sell user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to the developer or other remote servers.
